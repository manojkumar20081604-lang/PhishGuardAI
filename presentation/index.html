卍Under the Hood · The Bankai

A real model — trained, exported, embedded

Training pipeline

  • Live feeds: OpenPhish + PhishTank for positive class
  • Legit corpus: Majestic Million + Common Crawl
  • 10 URL features: length, HTTPS, IP-in-URL, entropy, dash count, subdomain depth, TLD age, digit ratio, special chars, suspicious TLD
  • Classifier: GradientBoosting (scikit-learn)
  • Export: ONNX format, ~KB-scale, runs on WebAssembly

Fusion & explainability

  • ML score blended with homoglyph flag, brand reputation, trust list, password-form heuristic
  • Verdict: SAFE / SUSPICIOUS / PHISHING
  • Every verdict carries plain-language reasons
  • Brand-safety gate: 58 high-value domains escalate on any anomaly
  • Model-age indicator warns at 90 / 180 days
url-model-v3.onnx is bundled with the extension. It loads once per browser session, runs on WebAssembly, and produces verdicts before a network request would even leave your machine.

卍Security Depth · Homoglyph Hunt

Catching what humans can't see

The fake Apple site

www.аррӏе.com

Looks identical to apple.com — but these letters are Cyrillic characters. Our homoglyph detector folds them back to ASCII and catches the clone instantly.

The real one

www.apple.com

Genuine internationalized domains are never flagged — false-positive safety is built into the detector's rules.

Punycode decoding

Raw punycode xn--80ak6aa92e is automatically folded back to its Unicode form for analysis. Many kits use this to hide Cyrillic / Greek substitutions.

Invisible characters

Zero-width joiners (U+200D), soft hyphens, RTL marks — caught by the same engine. Combined with the brand-safety gate, the verdict surfaces the actual brand being impersonated.
